# Commercial Licensing

Qingye X Console is released as source-available software.

The public repository allows personal, non-commercial evaluation. Commercial use requires a separate written license agreement from the copyright owner.

## Commercial Use Includes

- Using the software inside a company or paid team workflow.
- Offering the software as a hosted service or SaaS product.
- Selling access, subscriptions, support, setup, deployment, or managed hosting.
- Integrating the software into a paid product, paid crawler system, agency service, or client deliverable.
- White-labeling, redistributing, or reselling modified or unmodified versions.
- Using the software to generate revenue directly or indirectly.

## How To Request A Commercial License

Contact the copyright owner with:

- Company or individual name
- Contact email
- Intended use case
- Number of users or deployments
- Whether hosted/SaaS usage is required
- Whether redistribution or white-label rights are required
- Expected support requirements

## Suggested Commercial License Tiers

These tiers are suggestions only. Replace them with your final pricing before publishing.

| Tier | Scope | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual Commercial | 1 user, local use | For freelancers or solo operators |
| Team Commercial | Small internal team | Internal use only, no redistribution |
| Business Commercial | Company-wide or production use | Includes deployment rights |
| SaaS / Hosted | Hosted service or platform | Requires explicit SaaS rights |
| White-label / Redistribution | Resale or client distribution | Requires explicit redistribution rights |

## Important

No commercial rights are granted until a written agreement is signed by the copyright owner.

This document is not legal advice. Before monetizing or publicly licensing the project, have a qualified lawyer review the final license terms.
